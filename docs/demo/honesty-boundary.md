# Honesty boundary

DairyDNA is a **demonstration and portfolio project**. This page states
plainly what it is and is not, so nobody — presenter or audience — walks away
with the wrong impression.

## What DairyDNA is

- A local-only, single-process (or Aspire-composed) demo of a decision-support
  pipeline: synthetic data generation → forecasting → transportation costing
  → allocation optimization → what-if scenarios → historical replay/regret
  analysis → visualization.
- A vehicle for showing engineering practices: reproducible synthetic data,
  a real MIP solver (OR-Tools) behind a documented interface, ML.NET
  forecasting models with governance/versioning, OpenTelemetry
  instrumentation, and automated tests (unit/integration/contract/reproducibility).

## What DairyDNA is not

- **Not a source of real market data.** Every farm, facility, customer,
  order, price, and inventory row is generated by a seeded synthetic
  generator (`DairyDNA.DataGenerator`), optionally blended with a handful of
  real *public reference* series (003) for texture — never live trading or
  proprietary data.
- **Not trading or dispatch advice.** "Recommendations" are OR-Tools
  optimizer output over the synthetic dataset — a *suggested* feasible
  allocation given the modeled constraints. No trade has been executed, no
  truck has been dispatched, and no recommendation should be acted on in the
  real world.
- **Not production-secure.** There is no authentication, no authorization,
  no tenant isolation, no rate limiting, no secrets management beyond
  standard .NET configuration/user-secrets for local development. Every
  endpoint is open to anyone who can reach the process. **Do not expose this
  application to the public internet or use it with real business data.**
- **Not production-ready logistics.** See "R1 logistics simplifications" in
  [`hardening-notes.md`](./hardening-notes.md): no multi-stop truck routing,
  no real carrier rate cards, no driver hours-of-service rules, flat safety
  stock, straight-line-distance transport costing.
- **Not a claim about historical performance.** The replay/regret feature
  (012) compares the optimizer to two simple, deterministic baseline
  policies over the *same synthetic dataset* — it is a relative,
  proxy-economics comparison, not a backtest against real historical
  outcomes.

## Data classification labels

Every demo screen and API response that matters is labeled with one of:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Synthetic** | Generated data: farms, facilities, customers, orders, prices, inventory, trucks. |
| **Forecast** | ML.NET model output (supply/demand/price bands) — a statistical estimate with an explicit confidence interval, not a guarantee. |
| **Recommendation** | OR-Tools optimizer output — a suggested feasible allocation, never an executed trade or dispatch. |

If you build on this codebase for something real, replace the synthetic
generator with real data pipelines, add real authentication/authorization,
and have the optimization/forecasting outputs reviewed by someone who
understands the actual business and regulatory constraints before acting on
them.
